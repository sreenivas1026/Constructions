Clarity & Trust

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding our contracting terms, milestone inspections, and structural warranties.

How does SLTG Builders manage turnkey contracting projects?

Before breaking ground, we execute a registered Turnkey Construction Agreement with an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). We lock steel, cement, and fixture brands upfront, ensuring complete specification clarity and zero surprises during construction.

What is your stage-wise milestone inspection schedule?

Work is executed strictly in verified physical stages. You inspect and approve each completed milestone: Foundation Footings, Plinth Beam, Ground Slab Casting, First Floor Slab Casting, Brickwork & Plastering, Electrical/Plumbing Conduits, and Final Handover.

Can I physically inspect the East-Facing 133 Sq. Yards Prestige Duplex?

Yes, absolutely. The property is open daily for private walkthroughs. You can verify the structural slab thickness, cove LED false ceiling, Asian Paints Royale finish, and modular kitchen fittings. Use the scheduler above to book your preferred slot.

Do you assist with bank loan financing and municipal approvals?

Yes. We coordinate with leading financial institutions including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Our liaison team facilitates municipal building plan approvals, structural stability certificates, and title scrutiny dossiers.

What does the 10-Year Structural Bond cover?

Our 10-year structural bond covers foundation stability, RCC column and beam integrity, concrete slabs, and load-bearing framing. In addition, we provide a 1-year complimentary maintenance period for plumbing, electrical fittings, and water seepage inspections.

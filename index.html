Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to common questions about construction costs, contracts, and municipal compliance.

How do you guarantee zero cost escalation during construction?

Before breaking ground, we execute a legally binding Turnkey Construction Agreement with an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). We lock steel, cement, and brand brands upfront, meaning material price fluctuations in the open market never impact your contracted quote.

What are your stage-wise payment milestones?

We operate on an escrow-style milestone schedule. You only release payments after on-site verification of each phase: Foundation, Plinth Beam, Ground Slab Casting, First Floor Slab Casting, Brickwork & Plastering, Electrical/Plumbing Concealment, and Handover.

Can I visit the 133 Sq Yards East-Facing Duplex in person?

Yes, absolutely! The property is open for physical walkthroughs every day. You can inspect the structural slab, false ceiling, Royal Paint coats, and modular kitchen fittings. Use the scheduler above to book your slot.

Do you assist with bank home loans and municipal sanctions?

Yes. We have direct tie-ups with leading financial institutions including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Our legal and liaison team prepares the building plan approvals, structural stability certificates, and title scrutiny dossiers for quick sanctioning.

What does the 10-Year Structural Guarantee cover?

Our 10-year structural guarantee covers the foundation, RCC columns, beams, slabs, and structural load-bearing integrity. In addition, we provide a 1-year complimentary maintenance period for plumbing, electrical fittings, and water seepage inspection.

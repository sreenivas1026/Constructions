Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent, definitive answers regarding contract terms, escrow milestones, and structural guarantees.

How does SLTG Builders legally guarantee zero cost escalations?

Before breaking ground, we execute a legally registered Turnkey Construction Agreement with an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). We hedge and lock steel, cement, and fixture brands upfront, meaning open market price fluctuations never alter your contracted price.

What is your stage-wise milestone payment schedule?

Payments are linked strictly to physical on-site progress. You release funds only after inspecting and approving each completed milestone: Foundation Footings, Plinth Beam, Ground Slab Casting, First Floor Slab Casting, Brickwork & Plastering, Electrical/Plumbing Conduits, and Handover.

Can I physically inspect the East-Facing 133 Sq. Yards Prestige Duplex?

Yes, absolutely! The property is open daily for private walkthroughs. You can verify the structural slab thickness, cove LED false ceiling, Asian Paints Royale finish, and modular kitchen fittings. Use the VIP Concierge above to book your preferred slot.

Do you assist with bank financing and municipal approvals?

Yes. We have direct institutional tie-ups with leading banks including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Our legal and liaison wing handles municipal building plan approvals, structural stability certificates, and title scrutiny dossiers for accelerated sanctioning.

What does the 10-Year Structural Bond cover?

Our 10-year structural bond covers foundation stability, RCC column and beam integrity, concrete slabs, and load-bearing framing. In addition, we provide a 1-year complimentary maintenance period for plumbing, electrical fittings, and water seepage inspections.

07 / Clarity & Protocol

Frequently Asked Questions.

Transparent answers to common queries regarding contracting, timelines, and site visits.

What types of projects does SLTG Builders undertake?

We construct custom independent homes on client land, bespoke luxury villas and duplexes, and professional/commercial workspaces. We deliver projects across Unfurnished (Core Shell), Semi-Furnished, and Fully Furnished Turnkey packages according to client requirements.

Why are prices not listed directly on the website?

True architectural construction cannot be generalized with flat square-foot rates. Every project depends on plot soil strata, structural height (G+1 vs multi-level), setback regulations, municipal sanction fees, and bespoke finish selections. We provide an exact, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with zero hidden clauses after evaluating your specific plot.

Can I physically inspect your luxury duplex projects?

Yes, absolutely. Our signature luxury duplex is fully built and open for private walkthroughs. Use the 15-day scheduler above to pick a convenient date and time window, and our project manager will host you on-site for a complete architectural tour.

What structural materials and brands do you mandate?

We never compromise on structural integrity. We exclusively use Fe550D primary TMT rebar (Tata Tiscon / Jindal), grade 53/43 cement (UltraTech / ACC), kiln-burnt red clay bricks, Polycab FR copper wiring, and Jaquar / Kohler sanitaryware.

How does SLTG verify real estate land and open plots?

Our legal panel conducts exhaustive 30-year link document examinations, encumbrance certificate (EC) reviews, master plan zoning compliance checks (HMDA / DTCP), and title deed verifications before recommending any open plot or layout.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Depending on structural elevation and whether the project is Unfurnished, Semi-Furnished, or Fully Furnished, delivery typically ranges from 8 to 14 months from structural excavation. Each phase has milestone checkpoints to guarantee 100% on-time delivery.

07 / Clarity & Protocol

Frequently Asked Questions.

Transparent answers to common queries regarding contracting, timelines, and site visits.

How does SLTG Builders handle turnkey contracting on my land?

We handle every aspect from raw ground to final key handover: soil bearing tests, architectural 2D/3D blueprints, municipal sanction documentation, structural RCC framing, premium electrical and plumbing, and interior finishing. Everything is governed by milestone contracts and scheduled quality inspections.

Why are prices not listed directly on the website?

True architectural construction cannot be generalized with flat square-foot numbers. Every project depends on plot soil strata, structural height (G+1 vs G+3), setback requirements, municipal fees, and bespoke finish selections. We provide an exact, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with zero hidden clauses after evaluating your specific plot.

Can I physically inspect the 133 Sq. Yards East-Facing Duplex?

Yes, absolutely. The duplex is fully built and ready for private walkthroughs. Use the 15-day scheduler above to pick a convenient date and time window, and our project manager will host you on-site for a complete architectural tour.

What structural materials and brands do you mandate?

We never compromise on structural integrity. We exclusively use Fe550D primary TMT rebar (Tata Tiscon / Jindal), grade 53/43 cement (UltraTech / ACC), kiln-burnt red clay bricks, Polycab FR copper wiring, and Jaquar / Kohler sanitaryware.

How does SLTG verify real estate land and open plots?

Our legal panel conducts exhaustive 30-year link document examinations, encumbrance certificate (EC) reviews, master plan zoning compliance checks (HMDA / DTCP), and title deed verifications before recommending any open plot or layout.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

For a standard G+1 duplex (approx. 2,400 built-up sq.ft), our contractual delivery timeline is typically 8 to 10 months from the day of structural foundation excavation. Each phase has milestone checkpoints to guarantee 100% on-time delivery.
